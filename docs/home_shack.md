 ![An old picture of my shack](/assets/Radio-1.png "The old shack when I used an Icom 256 Pro II")
 
I now have a Remote Shack that has largely replaced the Home Shack. The following describes the Home Shack as it was.

## HF

- Elecraft K3S Transceiver, connected via a
- SG230 Antenna Coupler (now at remote shack) to an
- Inverted L at 10m.
- Occasionally, I use the K2 that I built back in the 2000's

## VHF/UHF

- Icom IC-7100  HF, VHF and UHF transceiver connected to a
- Diamond V2000 vertical white stick at 5m

## Also

- Elecraft KX3 for operation from the campervan
- Elecraft KX2 for SOTA
- Icom IC-E92D DSTAR VHF and UHF Handheld
- Yaesu FT-5D C4FM VHF and UHF Handheld

## Software

- Ham Radio Deluxe and Win4K3Suite - to control the K3S
- FL-Digi, WSJT-X, JTAlert - used for digital modes
