**I'm Gareth Howell, callsign M5KVK.**
Welcome to my github pages. This is the head page for a series of pages of information relating to my hobby as an amateur radio operator.

My main area of interest is the use of Digital Modes on HF. For this I use FLDigi, DM780, WSJT-X etc. I'm also using a variety of methods to improve my CW back to a state where I am prepared to venture back on the air.

I do a bit of mobile operation as well. I also do some construction. Some years ago I built an Elecraft K2. However, my main rig is now a Flex 6400.

## Station Overview

- **Main (Remote) Station:** Flexradio 6400 with End Fed Random Wire antenna tuned by SGC SG-230 Automatic ATU located at the base of the mast. For more info see [Remote Shack](/remote_shack.md)
- **Home Station:** Icom 7100 with 4m J-Pole, and 2m/70cm co-linear. See here for more info on the *old* [Home Shack](/home_shack.md)
- **Mobile Station:** Autocruise Rhythm campervan - Yaesu FTM-400D with Comet 2m/70cm antenna.

## Digital Voice & AllStar

- [Node 678500](https://m5kvk.org/678500) - 678500 is a Hub node
- [Node 678502](https://m5kvk.org/678502) - 678502 is an access-controlled link to the GB3OV repeater located near St Neots, Cambridgeshire, UK and serving an area bounded by Wellingborough to the West, Peterborough to the North, Cambridge to the East and Sandy to the South, with extended access North East into Fenland.
- [Node 678503](https://m5kvk.org/678503) - 678503 is a "private" node that sits on my bench with a speaker-mic to allow me to access AllStarLink without a radio.

## Packet & Messaging

- [GB7EAT](https://m5kvk.org/gb7eat) - Packet BBS located QTHR and accessible on 144.800MHz using AX.25 OR FX.25.
